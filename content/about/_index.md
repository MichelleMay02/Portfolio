I am an early-career environmental and Earth systems scientist with interdisciplinary training in ecosystem science, satellite remote sensing, and atmospheric dynamics. My academic work focuses on understanding Earth system processes across spatial and temporal scales, with particular emphasis on climate variability and large-scale atmospheric circulation.

My research interests lie at the intersection of physical climate science and environmental systems, with the goal of translating fundamental research into insights relevant for real-world environmental challenges.

## Academic Background

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Environmental and Earth Systems Science, with a concentration in Ecosystem Science, from the City College of New York (CCNY).

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at CCNY, where my research focuses on atmospheric circulation dynamics and climate system variability.

## Research Interests

My research interests include:
- Large-scale atmospheric circulation (including the Hadley Cell)
- Climate variability and long-term climate change
- Satellite and reanalysis data analysis
- Climate–ecosystem interactions

## Professional Motivation

I am motivated by the challenge of connecting Earth system science to real-world environmental decision-making. I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary research that supports data-driven approaches to sustainability, climate resilience, and environmental policy.
